# Tintenfunken

A print-ready deck of writing-craft cards for authors. Content is German; code and docs are English.

## Language

**Card** (German: _Karte_):
One writing-craft concept a writer can apply, whatever its Category. A Card is the concept, not its printed or on-screen form; see Visual representation.
_Avoid_: Item, entry, concept

**Deck**:
The whole set of Cards.
_Avoid_: Collection, set

**Index**:
The `cards.md` file that lists every Card grouped by Category and defines the Deck's order.
_Avoid_: Table of contents, manifest

## Card content

**Category**:
An aspect of writing that groups Cards; every Card belongs to exactly one, and it determines the Category color of the Card's Visual representation. Currently twelve (Archetyp, Wandlung, Struktur, Trope, Konflikt, Tempo, Weltenbau, Atmosphäre, Perspektive, Szene, Dialog, Thema).
_Avoid_: Type, tag, genre

**Number**:
A Card's position in the Index order, shown on its Visual representation.
_Avoid_: ID, position

**Title**:
The name of a Card, typically German, optionally followed by a well-known English synonym in parentheses, e.g. "Falsche Fährte (Red Herring)".
_Avoid_: Name, heading

**Artwork**:
The illustration of a Card, described by an Artwork prompt.

**Essence**:
The prose text that summarises the Card.
_Avoid_: Definition, description, front text

**Function** (German: _Funktion_):
What the Card does in a story.

**Application** (German: _Anwendung_):
How a writer applies the Card to their own writing, often phrased as questions.
_Avoid_: Prompt (reserved for Artwork prompt)

**Examples** (German: _Beispiele_):
Real, existing works, classic or pop-cultural, that illustrate the Card. Never invented.

**Pitfall** (German: _Warnung_):
A way the Card commonly goes wrong when used.
_Avoid_: Caveat, Vorsicht

**References** (German: _Passt zu_):
The Cards that go together with this one and are often combined with it in a story, within or across Categories, e.g. Der Mentorentod (Trope) references Der Mentor (Archetyp). Stronger than mere relatedness.
_Avoid_: Pairing, related card, link, tag

**Sources** (German: _Quellen_):
Where the Card comes from.

**Artwork prompt**:
The concise text used to generate a Card's Artwork. The prompt describes a low fidelity representation, naming no specific colors.
_Avoid_: Image prompt

## Presentation

**Visual representation**:
How a Card is shown, printed or on screen. It has a front and a back. The front shows title, Category, Artwork and Essence; the back shows the remaining Card content.
_Avoid_: Physical card, card (when the printed form is meant)

**Front** / **Back**:
The two sides of a Visual representation.

**Category color**:
The color that makes a Category recognizable at a glance on a Visual representation. Related Categories share a hue family, unrelated ones differ in hue, no two are confusable, and each stays dark enough for white text yet clearly not black.
_Avoid_: Accent color, card color, colour

**Card layout**:
How content is arranged on a Visual representation.

**Page layout**:
How Visual representations are arranged on the A4 sheets for printing.

**Overview** (German: _Übersicht_):
The view that shows the whole Deck at a glance, each Card as a miniature of its Front in Index order; selecting one opens it in the Preview.
_Avoid_: Gallery, grid, index (Index is the `cards.md` file)

**Preview** (German: _Vorschau_):
The view for browsing the Deck one Visual representation at a time, flipping between Front and Back.
_Avoid_: Viewer, browser

**Print view** (German: _Druck_):
The view that shows the whole Deck as A4 sheets in the Page layout, ready to print.
_Avoid_: Print preview, print mode

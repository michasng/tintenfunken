# Idea: Card "Das warnende Beispiel"

Status: needs-triage

## Request

Proposed new Card: a character who shows a figure the negative consequences of something, so the figure (and the reader) sees what it may become. Prime example: Gollum for Frodo.

Suggested References by the requester: [Poetische Gerechtigkeit](../../cards/poetische-gerechtigkeit.md), [Negativer Bogen](../../cards/negativer-bogen.md), [Vorausdeutung (Foreshadowing)](../../cards/vorausdeutung.md).

## Source

Brandon Sanderson, lecture on magic systems, section "Flaws and Limitations" (around 40:12–41:37). Relevant points:

- Limitations and costs of a power create more story potential than the power itself ("the flaws and limitations and the costs are where generally your story happens").
- _The Wheel of Time_: the more you use the power, the more likely you go insane and kill everyone you love. The prologue shows someone who has already done so, "so you understand the stakes and consequences right from the get-go".
- "It's the same thing as putting Gollum in your story so you can see what the character will become."

The rest of the excerpt (Superman's three story types, the TV anecdote, the three paradigms for magic stories) belongs to [Sandersons Zweites Gesetz](../../cards/sandersons-zweites-gesetz.md) and is not needed for this Card.

## Considerations

### Overlap with Kontrastfigur (Foil)

[Kontrastfigur (Foil)](../../cards/kontrastfigur.md) already describes a figure that embodies "den Weg, den der Protagonist nicht gewählt hat". Gollum is a classic Foil. A separate Card is justified only by a distinct focus:

- **Foil:** contrast; an alternative path that highlights the protagonist's traits.
- **New Card:** the protagonist's possible _future_; a warning that makes Stakes visible and foreshadows the fall.

The difference is real but narrow. YAGNI would suggest extending the Foil Card instead; a separate Card is preferred because its purpose (Stakes, foreshadowing) differs from characterization.

### Title

Candidates: "Das warnende Beispiel" (preferred, most self-explanatory), "Der Mahner", "Blick in die Zukunft".

### Category

**Wandlung**, next to Kontrastfigur and Negativer Bogen. Archetyp is possible, but this is a function rather than an archetype.

## Proposed content

### Essence

Eine Figur, die bereits den Preis gezahlt hat, den der Protagonist noch zahlen könnte. Sie zeigt, was aus ihm wird, wenn er seinen Weg fortsetzt.

### Funktion

Macht Einsätze und Kosten früh greifbar, deutet einen möglichen Fall voraus und gibt der Entscheidung des Protagonisten Gewicht.

### Anwendung

Was wird aus meiner Figur, wenn sie ihrem Fehler oder ihrer Macht nachgibt? Wer hat diesen Weg schon zu Ende gegangen, und wann begegnet meine Figur ihm?

### Beispiele

- Gollum für Frodo (Der Herr der Ringe)
- Lews Therin im Prolog von _Das Rad der Zeit_
- Marleys Geist für Scrooge (_Eine Weihnachtsgeschichte_)
- Darth Vader für Luke (Star Wars)

### Warnung

Gleicht die Warnfigur dem Helden zu sehr, wird sein Schicksal vorhersehbar: Entweder fällt er genauso, oder er entkommt auf billige Weise.

### Passt zu

Ordered by strength:

1. [Einsätze (Stakes)](../../cards/einsaetze.md): Sanderson's core point.
2. [Vorausdeutung (Foreshadowing)](../../cards/vorausdeutung.md)
3. [Negativer Bogen](../../cards/negativer-bogen.md): the warning figure has completed it.
4. [Sandersons Zweites Gesetz](../../cards/sandersons-zweites-gesetz.md): it shows the cost of power.
5. [Kontrastfigur (Foil)](../../cards/kontrastfigur.md): for distinction.

[Poetische Gerechtigkeit](../../cards/poetische-gerechtigkeit.md) fits only weakly: the warning figure may embody punishment for its vice, but the Card aims at something else. Optional.

### Quellen

Candidate: "a traveler on a path meeting a hunched, ragged version of himself coming the other way"
